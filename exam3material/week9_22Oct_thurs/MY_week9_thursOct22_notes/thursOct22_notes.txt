Revisit our example:

We want to prove that for any positive integer n:

    1 + 2 + ... + n = n*(n+1)/2

    Above claim is P(n)


Proof by induction.

    Base case! Domain?: Natural numbers. We want to prove that the claim holds for the base case (1).
    We have that the left side of the claim is 1, and the right side of the claim is 1(1+1)/2 = 1. 
    Because both sides are equal, the base case holds.

    Inductive step: We assume the inductive hypothesis  - such that
        1 + 2 + 3 + ... + k = k(k+1)/2
        for some fixed positive integer k. We want to show that our claim holds for k+1.
        We want to show that:
        1 + 2 + 3 + ... + k + (k+1) = (k+1)((k+1)+1)/2

    We have that:
        1 + 2 + 3 + ... + k + (k+1) = k(k+1)/2 + (k+1) --- by inductive hypothesis
                                    = k(k+1) + 2(k+1)/2 --- rewrite (k+1) in a common denominator
                                    = ( k + 1 )( k + 2 )/2 --- factor out a (k+1) term from both
                                    = ( k + 1 )( (k+1) + 1 ) / 2 --- rewrite (k+2) as a (k+1) + 1

    Therefore, the claim holds for (k+1).

    We have proved the base case and the inductive step, so our claim holds by induction for all 
    positive integers n.

Example: Prove that 3^n - 1 is even for all positive integers n.


what does "even" mean? divisible by 2, modulo 2 == 0
    {x: x = 2k, k in Z, k >= 1}



Proof by induction.

Base case. We must prove that our claim holds for the smallest positive integer, n = 1. We have that 3^1 - 1 = 2. 
Since 2*1 where 1 is an integer, we have that 2 is even, the base case holds.

Inductive step. --- Assume the inductive hypothesis, such that: 3^k - 1 is even for some fixed positive integer k.
We want to prove that 3^(k+1) - 1 is also even.

=3^(k+1) - 1
=(3 * 3^k) - 1
=3(3^k - 1) - 1 + 3
=3(3^k-1) + 2 is still even!

Since 3^k - 1 is even, we have 3^k - 1 = 2a, where a is an integer.
We have that:
    3^(k+1) - 1 = (3*3^k) - 1
                = (3 * 3^k) - 1
                = 3 (3^k - 1) - 1 + 3
                = 3 (3^k-1) + 2
                = 3 (2a) + 2
                = 6a + 2
                = 2 * (3a + 1)

And so, 3^(k+1) - 1 = 2(3a+1), where 3a + 1 is an integer.
